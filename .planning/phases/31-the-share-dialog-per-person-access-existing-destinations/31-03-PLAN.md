## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|--------------|
| Owner's revoke action -> revoked member's own client | The server's DELETE on `collection_keys` is the authoritative fact; the revoked member's client must lose access through its OWN normal, already-authorized sync channel, never through a push naming them their own removal. |

| T-31-11 | Information Disclosure | The revoked member's own WS channel | low | accept | `collections::revoke_access` (existing, unchanged) deliberately publishes no event naming the just-revoked member their own removal — inherited, already-audited SHARE-06 behavior, not new to this plan. |
