<objective>
Give every account — owner, plain member, or standalone — a real, honest "Delete account" surface. The
owner sees an explicit, specific warning naming their family and how many people lose access; a plain
member's own account deletion silently re-keys their collections through the same path as an explicit
removal, then signs them out.

Purpose: FAM-10 needs a real UI trigger. CONTEXT.md's owner-self-deletion decision — the confirmation
must say, in plain words, that deleting the account ends the family for everyone in it — is this plan's
one non-negotiable copy requirement, symmetric to Plan 25-08's UX-04 honesty warning.
Output: `SecurityTab.tsx`'s new "Delete account" section and a new `DeleteAccountDialog.tsx`.
</objective>
