<objective>
Layer 05-UI-SPEC.md's three small, deliberately-quiet UI surfaces on top of Plan 05-03's now-live client sync engine: a reconnecting-only presence dot, a proactive live-edit-conflict banner that never clobbers unsaved typing, and a calm info-toast + auto-close when the open item is deleted elsewhere. This is the plan that makes SYNC-03's multi-device experience actually VISIBLE to a user, without turning it chatty (05-CONTEXT.md's explicit ambient-not-chatty framing, reaffirmed by 05-UI-SPEC.md's own "exactly three small UI additions" framing).

Both new detection mechanisms in this plan (remote delete, proactive conflict) are derived from state the app ALREADY re-renders reactively (`page.tsx`'s `items.find(...)` derivation of `selectedItem`, and `DetailPanel`'s already-live `item` prop) — neither needs a new subscription to Plan 05-03's sync engine, keeping this plan's diff small and its behavior easy to reason about.

Purpose: complete SYNC-03's user-facing half — conflicts and cross-device deletions are surfaced honestly and calmly, never silently.
Output: a working sync-status dot, an extended DetailPanel with a second (proactive) conflict-banner trigger path, and a new info-toast variant, all covered by component/unit tests.
</objective>
