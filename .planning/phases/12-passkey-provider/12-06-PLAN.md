Close the two defects the adversarial re-review found in 12-05's consent refactor, both on the LOCKED-vault path:
1. NEW BLOCKER — the locked-vault consent screen never displays: App.tsx only checks the pending-ceremony key once at mount, so the payload the background writes AFTER unlock is never read; every locked-vault create()/get() silently falls through to native, defeating Decision A on that path.

The robust fix for #2 is the early-ack pattern the page-bridge comment explicitly (and wrongly) rejected: the extension, once it accepts a request, becomes the sole authority on when to fall through. This also removes the "user sees both prompts" artifact.
