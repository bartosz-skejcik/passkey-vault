<objective>
Close 27-VERIFICATION.md Blocker 1 (the E2-error backstop is disconfirmed): give `vault-store.ts`'s
`pendingSharedItems` array a pending-vs-broken discriminant computed from the exact signal
`decryptItemRow` already derives internally (`CollectionKeyPendingError` vs. a generic decrypt
failure, itself gated on `collections-store.ts`'s `hasRefreshedThisSession()`), plumb that
discriminant through the `vault.list` message boundary, and make `ItemListView.tsx`'s pending-row
loop degrade a "broken" entry into a visible, honest warning treatment instead of an indefinite
neutral shimmer. Preserves 27-04's own prohibition (a shared row the caller has access to is never
silently dropped with no trace) and 27-04's own drop discipline (no VaultItem is retained for a
never-decrypted row) -- this plan does not reintroduce a retained-copy shape; it only teaches the
ALREADY-SURFACED pending-row channel to tell the two states apart and render them honestly.

Purpose: the phase built three consumers of `undecryptable: true` (ItemListView's degraded row,
ItemDetailView's warning banner, the ceremony's defensive filter) and no producer for the ONE
channel that actually carries every shared-item failure (`pendingSharedItems`). Rather than
retrofit a "retained last-known-good copy" the extension's own architecture deliberately does not
keep (27-04's explicit choice, preserved here), this plan makes `pendingSharedItems` itself carry
the discriminant and gives the E2 skeleton row a second, terminal visual state.
Output: `vault-store.ts`'s `PendingSharedItemEntry.status`, a real-WASM proof of the classification,
`ItemListView.tsx`'s broken-row rendering, and an honest `sync.itemUndecryptableWarning` string.
</objective>

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-27-26 | Information Disclosure | `vault.list`'s `pending[].status` field | low | accept | The new field is a two-valued enum computed from local decrypt-attempt bookkeeping -- carries no plaintext, no key material, no collection membership info beyond what `pending[].collectionId` already exposed pre-existing. | T-27-27 | Tampering | popup-side rendering of `status` | low | accept | The popup only ever RECEIVES this field from the background's own response to its own `vault.list` request; the popup has no write path into `pendingSharedItems` and cannot forge a classification the background didn't compute. |
| T-27-28 | Denial of Service | `markPending`'s upsert loop | low | accept | Bounded by the number of distinct shared-item ids the caller has access to (already bounded by the existing `pendingSharedItems` array); the upsert is a linear scan over that same small array, no new unbounded growth introduced. |

No package-manager installs in this plan (`T-{phase}-SC` not applicable).
