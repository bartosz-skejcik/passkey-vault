already fetches and renders the exact co-recipient rows a revoke button acts on — this is genuinely

crypto-plumbing task (no unwrap-and-reseal-an-existing-collection's-key path exists client-side yet),
not cheap alongside revoke.

Purpose: today a shared collection's membership is effectively write-once — you can create a share and
never adjust it. A revoke affordance makes membership genuinely editable in the one direction that
matters for access control: taking it away.
Output: `revokeCollectionAccess`/`revokeItemShare` in `web/src/lib/vault/api.ts`; a new
`RevokeShareDialog.tsx`; a wired row action on both `SharingOverviewPanel.tsx` tabs; live-proven against
a real second recipient.
