<objective>
Wire the lazy-reseal fallback's TRIGGER: on every unlock and every subsequent sync pull that
discovers a resealable grant, call `reshareCollectionToNewMember` (30-04) for each pending pair the
current session can act on -- mirroring `identity/ensure.ts`'s existing `publishOnUnlock` cadence,
deliberately including the sharer's own session (30-DECISION-FSH-02.md's confirmed refinement, not
scoped to "some other member").

Purpose: this is the mechanism that actually delivers a family-wide key to a newcomer whose invite
was generated before the share existed -- FSH-02's central claim.
Output: `runFamilyWideResealTrigger`; wired into `store.ts`'s existing `syncCallbacks`.
</objective>
