<objective>
Add the `passkey-rs` (1Password) soft ES256 WebAuthn authenticator to a new sibling crate `crates/pv-provider`, implement a vault-backed `CredentialStore` adapter, and expose two new `pv-wasm` bindings — re-exported through `extension/lib/crypto/wasm-loader.ts`, the sole sanctioned importer of `./wasm/pv_wasm.js` — that let the background service worker create/read provider passkeys WITHOUT ever letting the plaintext private key material surface as a JS-visible value — reusing `encrypt_item`/`decrypt_item` unchanged (D-07).

D-08 note: the previously planned ephemeral-wrap module (`crates/pv-core/src/provider.rs`, `INFO_PROVIDER_CRED_KEY`) is DE-SCOPED per D-19 (audit S3: re-encrypting an already-encrypted `EncryptedItem` with a seed stored next to the ciphertext adds no confidentiality). This phase therefore introduces ZERO new HKDF domain-separation contexts — D-08 is satisfied vacuously; record that explicitly in SUMMARY.md.

Purpose: This is the phase's crypto foundation. Every later plan (background orchestration, MAIN-world bridge, popup UI) calls into these bindings; getting the zero-knowledge property right here (no plaintext private key crossing the WASM->JS boundary as a return value) is what makes the rest of the phase's `/gsd-secure-phase` gate (D-15) passable.
Output: `crates/pv-provider` (new workspace member), two new `#[wasm_bindgen]` functions in `crates/pv-wasm/src/lib.rs`, their re-exports in `extension/lib/crypto/wasm-loader.ts`, full `cargo test` coverage for the round-trip create->encrypt->decrypt->assert flow.
</objective>
