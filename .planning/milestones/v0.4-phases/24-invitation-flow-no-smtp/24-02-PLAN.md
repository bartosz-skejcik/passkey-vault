<objective>
Build the actual `/api/invitations/*` surface — the wiring RESEARCH.md calls "almost entirely reuse":
create (owner-only), fetch-metadata (no session, but proof-gated per 24-CONTEXT.md's Amendment 2),
accept (optional session, the phase's one deliberately low-trust write surface, also proof-gated), and

into the collection-scoped branch, rate-limiting, and the unified-failure-cause guarantee.

Purpose: this is where FAM-04/05/06 become real, callable, tested server behavior — and where
Amendment 2's proof-of-possession leg closes the gap that would otherwise let `invite_id` alone (no
secret needed) join the family.

`tests/invitations.rs` covering the happy paths and the documented edge cases.
</objective>
