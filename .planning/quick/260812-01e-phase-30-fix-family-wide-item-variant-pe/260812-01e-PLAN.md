<objective>
Closes the one remaining gap in Phase 30's SC2 (`.planning/phases/30-the-living-group-family-wide-sharing/30-VERIFICATION.md`): the family-wide ITEM variant is broken, not merely unproven. Every family's family-wide items today route through ONE singleton `item_bucket` collection carrying exactly one `family_wide_access_level`, while `ShareDialog` offers a level control per share. A non-creator member fanned out below `edit` gets a hard 403 on `vault::move_item`'s destination gate and an impossible-retry `share.createFailed`; after the family's first item share, the level control is silently a no-op (`findOrCreateFamilyItemBucket` ignores its own `level` argument, and `grantCollectionToRecipients` swallows the resulting 409 as success).

**LOCKED decision 1** (`260812-01e-CONTEXT.md`): a family may hold up to THREE `item_bucket` collections, one per access level. Whoever CONTRIBUTES an item to a bucket holds `edit` on that bucket for themselves, while every other member holds the bucket's own declared level -- the same asymmetry `collections::create` already establishes for a collection's creator, generalized from "the creator" to "any contributor." This is accepted and must never be hidden.

**Revision note (post plan-check, `260812-01e-PLAN-CHECK.md`, verdict REVISE, 6 blockers):** the first draft's diagnosis and file-level accuracy held; its mechanism did not. Granting a contributor `edit` via a `move_item`-side-effect is reachable by ANY member holding any row on a bucket (two API calls: create a junk personal item, move it in -- no real "contribution" is enforced, and LOCKED decision 1's own "has ever contributed" language accepts this is permanent). That `edit` row, left unbounded, silently also conferred (a) the power to revoke every OTHER member from the bucket via `collections::revoke_access`'s bare `RequireEdit` gate, and (b) the power to hand OTHER members or invitees a level higher than the bucket's own declared one via `add_member`/the invite fold-in's `may_grant_access_level`-only bound -- reopening the exact "propagator's own level substituted for the share's declared level" bug shape migration 0020/CR-01 exists to close. This revision keeps the locked mechanism (decision 1's row is not renegotiable) and adds Task 2 to close both side doors as ADDITIONAL bounds layered on top of the existing, untouched nine-arm `may_grant_access_level` match -- LOCKED decision 2 stays intact. It also fixes Task 6's (now Task 8's) vacuous Face-2 assertion and adds the UI-copy-honesty task LOCKED decision 1 explicitly requires ("if any UI copy would now be false, fix the copy").

Purpose: restore FSH-01's full "share a folder OR AN ITEM with the whole family" claim, with recipient-side proof through real crypto (never mocked), per LOCKED decision 3 -- and without opening a privilege-escalation path decision 1 never sanctioned.

Output: a per-level item_bucket schema, a server-side mechanism that atomically claims `edit` for a contributing member on the same request that writes their item in, two closed privilege-escalation side doors, level-keyed client resolution, a defended 409-swallow, an excluded item_bucket from the ordinary folder tab, honest UI copy, and a live Playwright proof that genuinely falsifies both Face 1 and Face 2 -- each new claim backed by a falsification-proven test (revert, observe RED, restore, observe GREEN, record the exact output).
